## Lumenary Scientia: the sciences, always free and open source

Lumenary Scientia is Lumenary Academy's school of the sciences. Everything in it is, and will always be, free and open source: the code under Apache-2.0 and the lessons, exercises and diagrams under CC BY-SA 4.0. There are no accounts, no advertising and no paid tiers. Anyone may use, translate, adapt and share it.

Mathematics is the first subject, and it is open now. Its curriculum runs from counting toward the first year of graduate school, and every topic on the curriculum map has a first-draft lesson. Every lesson was written with AI assistance and is still awaiting review by a human mathematician (see [About](../about/)). The other subjects will follow, built the same way and under the same licences.

## Lumenary Prep: exam preparation (paid)

Lumenary Prep, at [prep.lumenary.academy](https://prep.lumenary.academy), prepares learners for exams: college admissions (the SAT, ACT and CLT), graduate and professional school (the GRE General Test and Subject Tests, GMAT and LSAT), English-language tests (IELTS and TOEFL iBT), and professional licences (the Enrolled Agent exam, the U.S. Tax Court nonattorney exam and the CPA Exam).

Lumenary Prep is a paid service. Each course has one price, paid once, for lifetime access. Its facts come from each exam's official sources, and its lessons and practice questions are original writing. Its courses are labelled as AI-drafted until a qualified person has reviewed them, and it makes no score guarantees or pass-rate claims.

Lumenary Prep is separate from Lumenary Scientia. Paying for Prep is never needed to use Scientia, and Scientia stays free and open source.
